About Me

I am a final-year undergraduate in Software Engineering at Hangzhou City University and a Visiting Scholar at Duke-NUS Medical School, Singapore, working with Prof. Nan Liu.

I am also interested in computer-use agents and LLM evaluation frameworks, and am part of the startup Touchstone AI.

Research Interests

My research interests lie in medical artificial intelligence, computer-use agents, and high-performance computing. I work on building data, evaluation frameworks, and efficient systems for AI.

  • Medical AI. Clinical dialogue simulation, synthetic healthcare data, and multimodal diagnostic reasoning evaluation.
  • Agents and efficient systems. Cross-platform computer-use benchmarks, GPU inference optimization, and high-performance computing.
